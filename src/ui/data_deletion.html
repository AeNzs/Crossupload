User Data Deletion Instructions

How to remove your connected accounts, OAuth tokens, and uploaded media from CrossUpload.

1. Remove Accounts Inside CrossUpload

Sign in to CrossUpload at {{APP_URL}}, open the Kredensial panel using the key icon in the top-right header, select the provider (YouTube, TikTok, or Meta), and click Putuskan on any connected account. This permanently deletes the encrypted OAuth tokens and associated job records from the database.

2. Delete Uploaded Videos

Open the Upload page and click Hapus next to any item in the video library to remove both the original video file and its generated thumbnail from disk.

3. Revoke Access at the Platform Level

You can also revoke CrossUpload's permissions directly in your TikTok Security settings, Google Account Third-Party Connections, or Facebook Business Integrations settings. For manual deletion assistance, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
