Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 · Service URL: {{APP_URL}}

1. Information We Access and Store

When you connect a TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, or Facebook account via OAuth 2.0, CrossUpload collects only the minimum information required to publish your videos: your platform account identifier, public display name or channel title, and OAuth access and refresh tokens. CrossUpload also stores video files, captions, hashtags, and publishing schedules that you explicitly upload.

2. How Information Is Used

Your credentials and media files are used exclusively to authenticate with the connected platform APIs, normalize video format via FFmpeg, and upload videos to the destinations and times you choose. CrossUpload never sells, rents, or shares user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Storage and Security

CrossUpload runs as a self-hosted application. All OAuth tokens and API Client Secrets are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM inside a local SQLite database and are never returned in plain text to the browser. Temporary transcoded files are deleted automatically after each publishing job finishes.

4. TikTok, YouTube, and Meta API Compliance

CrossUpload's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. For TikTok, CrossUpload requests only the scopes needed to identify your account and upload or publish videos you initiate (`user.info.basic`, `video.upload`, `video.publish`). For Meta, CrossUpload accesses only Pages and linked Instagram Professional accounts you explicitly authorize.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You control all stored data directly. You can delete any uploaded video from the Upload library, disconnect any platform account to immediately erase its tokens, or revoke access directly in your TikTok, Google, or Facebook account settings. Detailed instructions are available on our Data Deletion page.
